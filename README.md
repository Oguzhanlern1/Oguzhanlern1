**Frontend & Mobile Developer · Founder of Olerna**

I turn ideas into digital products with a distinct visual identity and a clear purpose. My work brings together art direction, interface design, and software development to create websites and mobile experiences that people enjoy using and businesses can build on.

## Olerna

I founded [Olerna](https://olerna.com/) as an independent digital product studio with a global ambition. Our goal is to contribute to the global market by turning original ideas into expressive digital work and developing them into functional products.

An idea can start with a sketch, a business challenge, or a better way to handle an everyday task. At Olerna, we shape those ideas into interfaces with a recognizable identity, then build the workflows that make them useful. That means giving the same attention to typography, motion, and layout as we give to how people navigate, complete a task, and return to the product.

## What I bring to a product

- **Design to development** · I translate Figma designs into responsive interfaces and build reusable components that preserve the visual direction across screens.
- **Product workflows** · I build dashboards, authentication flows, and data-heavy interfaces, including the listing and pagination features people rely on in day-to-day work.
- **Web and mobile** · I develop for the web with React and Next.js and build mobile experiences with React Native and Expo.

## Professional experience

Over two years in frontend development, I worked on production websites and admin tools, implemented sign-in flows, and built interfaces for browsing and managing data. I also contributed to a mobile application for a large-scale event. That experience informs how I approach independent work, from the first design decisions through the details people encounter in the finished product.
